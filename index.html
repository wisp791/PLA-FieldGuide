Pokemon Legends: Arceus

Pokemon Legends: Arceus is an action RPG set in ancient Hisui, where you explore open regions, study wild Pokemon, complete the first regional Pokedex, and battle using a faster style-based turn system.

The game focuses on field research, catching, crafting, requests, and discovering how familiar Pokemon fit into a much earlier era of the Sinnoh region.
