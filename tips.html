Tips and Tricks

Practical habits, farms, and fight prep.

Field Habits

Do this

  • Carry Smoke Bombs, Sticky Globs, Revives, and several ball families.
  • Use Zisu's Training Grounds to patch movepools before hard fights.
  • Enter distortions unless you are protecting a shiny spawn.
  • Report research to Laventon after every expedition so Star Rank actually updates.

Avoid this

  • Assuming held items, trades, or abilities work like mainline games.
  • Using Strong Style before checking the turn order preview.
  • Buying every ball instead of crafting from field materials.
  • Ignoring pasture releases, since bulk releases feed Grit item farming.

Farming Routes

Alpha Blissey XP

  • Farm the Alpha Blissey near Obsidian Falls in Obsidian Fieldlands.
  • Use a physical Fighting attacker if possible. Close Combat users are ideal because Blissey has very low Defense.
  • Return to Jubilife Village and re-enter the region to refresh the route.

Regional alpha loops

  • Pick one region and sweep every fixed alpha you can safely beat or catch.
  • Battle for XP and item drops, or catch alphas for later release and Grit returns.
  • Sell excess valuables from the run and keep high-tier Grit for main team Effort Levels.

Star Piece crafting

  • Distortions drop colored shards and Stardust.
  • Craft Star Pieces with 3 Red Shards, 3 Blue Shards, 3 Green Shards, and 1 Stardust.
  • Sell Star Pieces instead of selling shards separately.

Training Grounds

  • Use Ingo at the Jubilife Village Training Grounds for repeatable trainer battles once unlocked.
  • Bring one strong lead and carry weaker party members for shared experience.
  • Use repeat battles for move mastery when wild routes feel slow.

Spoiler Fight Prep

General Noble habits

  • Dodge into or through attacks instead of backing straight away.
  • Throw balms only after charge, jump, or explosion recovery windows.
  • Use the battle phase to force a long balm window.

Type answers

  • Kleavor: Water, Rock, or Steel.
  • Hisuian Lilligant: Flying is best; Fire, Psychic, Poison, Ice, and Fairy also work.
  • Hisuian Arcanine: Water, Ground, or Fighting.
  • Hisuian Electrode: Fire, Ice, Poison, or Bug. Ground is defensive utility, not a Grass answer.
  • Hisuian Avalugg: Fighting or Steel.

Volo preparation

  • Bring a full team around level 65-70+ with raised Effort Levels.
  • Stock Max Revives and Full Restores before starting the fight.
  • Fairy coverage is important for Spiritomb and Garchomp.

Volo and Giratina plan

  • Do not spend too many resources early, because the fight continues into Giratina without a normal reset.
  • Use fast neutral damage to finish weakened targets before they take extra turns.
  • Keep at least one bulky Pokemon healthy for emergency healing turns.
