Requests Guide

Numbering correction: Main game has 94 requests. Daybreak adds requests 95-122. Shaymin is Request 92, Darkrai is Request 93, and Incarnate Forces of Hisui is Request 94.

Legendary & Mythical Pokemon

Shiny Hunting Odds

  • Base shiny roll is 1/4096.
  • Research Level 10 adds 1 extra roll; perfect dex entry adds 2 extra rolls.
  • Shiny Charm adds 3 extra rolls and requires Research Level 10 on the required dex entries. Darkrai and Shaymin are exempt.
  • Static legendaries and mythicals are shiny locked.

Post-Game & Arceus

Completion checklist

  • Catch every required species for the Azure Flute path.
  • Raise the required Pokedex entries to Research Level 10 for Shiny Charm. Darkrai and Shaymin are not required.
  • Finish requests for full completion, including Daybreak if installed.
  • Prepare level 70+ team with high Effort Levels for Arceus.

Arceus fight

  • Level 75, Normal by default, can shift plates during the action battle.
  • Dodge rings and beams, throw balms during recovery windows.
  • Lucario, Ursaluna, Garchomp, Togekiss, Hisuian Goodra, and Cresselia are comfortable picks.
