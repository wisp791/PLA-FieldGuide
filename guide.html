Game Overview & Core Differences

What changed

  • Pokemon appear directly in the overworld.
  • Many targets can be caught without battle.
  • Agile and Strong Style alter turn order, power, and PP cost.
  • Obedience is tied to Star Rank.
  • No held item system and no functional battle abilities.
  • Space-time distortions spawn rare Pokemon and valuable items.

Best habits

  • Report research to Laventon after every expedition.
  • Craft balls and stealth tools before shopping.
  • Use Smoke Bombs, Sticky Globs, food lures, and back strikes.
  • Release duplicates in pasture batches for Grit items.

Catching Mechanics

Stealth loop

  • Crouch before detection range.
  • Use grass, terrain, Smoke Bombs, and food.
  • Throw from behind for a back strike.

Stun tools

  • Mud Balls and Spoiled Apricorns work early.
  • Sticky Globs become the best general stun item.
  • Stun plus Gigaton Ball is strong for alphas.

Alphas

  • Fixed alpha spawns can be shiny in PLA.
  • Caught alphas keep size and elevated effort levels.
  • High level captures may disobey until Star Rank catches up.

Battle System

Styles

  • Normal: standard power and timing.
  • Agile: lower power, faster action speed, higher PP cost.
  • Strong: higher power, slower action speed, higher PP cost.

Use cases

  • Agile for status, finishing targets, or stealing turns.
  • Strong for one-hit KOs when the counterattack risk is acceptable.
  • Read the timeline before committing.

Star Rank
