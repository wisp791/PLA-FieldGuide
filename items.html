Poke Ball Guide

Grit & Effort Levels

System

  • Effort Levels replace EVs and IVs in practical team building.
  • Each stat can reach EL 10. There is no combined cap.
  • Grit Dust covers 0-3, Gravel 3-6, Pebble 6-9, Rock 9-10.

Farming

  • Catch outbreaks and release duplicates in bulk.
  • Alpha releases can yield high-tier Grit.
  • Prioritize main attacking stat, Speed, HP, then defenses.

Money

  • Big Nugget: 20,000
  • Comet Shard: 15,000
  • Nugget: 10,000
  • Pearl String: 10,000
  • Star Piece: 5,000

XP

  • Distortions for candy.
  • High-level alphas for battle XP.
  • Training Grounds for move mastery.

Rare items

  • Distortions for fossils, Porygon line, Magnemite line, Johto Sneasel, Weavile, and evolution items.
  • Ursaluna digging for Peat Block, stones, shards, and sellables.
  • Lost satchels for Merit Points.
